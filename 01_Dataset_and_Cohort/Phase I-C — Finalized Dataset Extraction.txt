7. Important Protocol Decision

For this first-generation digital twin study:

We should not attempt to model every cardiovascular variable.

The primary endpoint should be:

Anatomically constrained mechanical adaptation under altered gravity.

Therefore:

Primary outputs:

LV volume change
stroke volume change
wall stress
myocardial strain
pressure-volume relationship
ventricular remodeling index
