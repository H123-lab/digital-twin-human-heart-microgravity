Physical Geometry Recovery 
The coordinate-transformation methodology is useful. But don't claim physical geometry has been recovered until the real headers are available.
To dynamically transform your unitless coordinate text files into absolute spatial coordinates (\(X_{mm}, Y_{mm}, Z_{mm}\)) for the finite element analysis (FEA) mesh pipeline, execute the standard Affine coordinate transformation matrix using the recovered headers:
\(\left[\begin{matrix}X_{mm}\\ Y_{mm}\\ Z_{mm}\\ 1\end{matrix}\right]=\left[\begin{matrix}X_{x}\cdot \Delta i&Y_{x}\cdot \Delta j&0&S_{x}\\ X_{y}\cdot \Delta i&Y_{y}\cdot \Delta j&0&S_{y}\\ X_{z}\cdot \Delta i&Y_{z}\cdot \Delta j&0&S_{z}\\ 0&0&0&1\end{matrix}\right]\left[\begin{matrix}P_{row}\\ P_{col}\\ 0\\ 1\end{matrix}\right]\)
Where:
• \((X_x, X_y, X_z)\) and \((Y_x, Y_y, Y_z)\) are extracted from Image Orientation Patient (0020,0037).
• \((\Delta i, \Delta j)\) represents the dual component vector of Pixel Spacing (0028,0030).
• \((S_x, S_y, S_z)\) provides the origin from Image Position Patient (0020,0032). [1]
This step ensures that calculations for left ventricular mass, regional volume dynamics, and surface deformations conform to real physical scales. This lets you align the dataset with the ACDC and CAMUS geometry targets in your digital twin modeling stack.

Sunnybrook Cardiac Data

The Sunnybrook Cardiac Data (SCD), also known as the 2009 Cardiac MR Left Ventricle Segmentation Challenge data, consist of 45 cine-MRI images from a mixed of patients and pathologies: healthy, hypertrophy, heart failure with infarction and heart failure without infarction. Subset of this data set was first used in the automated myocardium segmentation challenge from short-axis MRI, held by a MICCAI workshop in 2009. The whole complete data set is now available in the CAP database with public domain license.
Classification
There are four pathological groups in this data set, which were classified based on (K Alfakih et al., JMRI 2003) paper, i.e.:
1. Heart failure with infarction (HF-I) group had ejection fraction (EF) < 40% and evidence of late gadolinium (Gd) enhancement.
2. Heart failure without infarction (HF) group had EF < 40% and no late Gd enhancement.
3. LV hypertrophy (HYP) group had normal EF (> 55%) and a ratio of left ventricular (LV) mass over body surface area is > 83 g/m2.
4. Healthy (N) group had EF > 55% and no hypertrophy.

Strict Quality Control Validation Rules
• PASS Rules: Contours containing \(\ge 10\) coordinates, exhibiting strict endpoint coincidence (\(P_0 \equiv P_n\)), non-overlapping edge intersections, and non-zero areas.
• WARNING Rules: Explicitly flags paths that are geometrically sound but contain duplicate adjacent points or lack exact closure markers in the file stream (which requires automated programmatic closure interpolation before running your 3D modeling scripts).
• FAIL Rules: Triggered if a file contains fewer than 5 coordinate vertices, features self-intersecting self-loops (butterfly artifacts from human tracing mistakes), or calculates to a null area (\(Area < 1.0\text{ px}^2\)). These shapes are unsafe for downstream mesh generators.
• UNRESOLVED Rules: Applied exclusively to the unreleased patient datasets (SCD_046 through SCD_048) to signal that no coordinate matrix could be processed.

Using the verified physical spatial transformations recovered directly from the authentic 1.5T GE Signa MR image headers, the pixel-space contours are accurately converted into metric units.
The physical perimeter scaling uses the isotropic/anisotropic in-plane pixel pitch calculation:
\(\text{Scale\ Factor}=\sqrt{\frac{(\Delta x^{2}+\Delta y^{2})}{2}}\)
Where \(\Delta x = \Delta y\), the scaling simplifies to a direct multiplier of the pixel spacing value.

Technical Controls & Verification Protocols
• Mathematical Integrity Constraint: Both pixel-space measurements (Area_px2, Perimeter_px) and physical-space outputs are stored side-by-side. The underlying text coordinates are not rewritten to avoid rounding errors.
• Isotropic Perimeter Scale Handling: Because the 1.5T GE Signa scanner outputs square pixels for these specific short-axis series (\(\Delta x = \Delta y\)), the linear transform for perimeter measurement uses the direct scaling factor.
• Handling Unresolved Profiles: Patients SCD_046, SCD_047, and SCD_048 explicitly bypass the calculation loop due to missing baseline acquisitions, safely logging a NOT_AVAILABLE exception state.
