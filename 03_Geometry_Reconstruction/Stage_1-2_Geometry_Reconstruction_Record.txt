This stage establishes the provenance, source inventory, physical imaging metadata, mask-derived geometry, and computational readiness of the five-patient ACDC DCM pilot cohort.

The purpose of this stage is to ensure that no synthetic, inferred, or unsupported imaging values are introduced before three-dimensional reconstruction and finite-element modeling.

The Stage 1 workflow distinguishes information into four categories:

VERIFIED: directly supported by available source/audit records.
CALCULATED: mathematically derived from verified quantities.
MODEL ASSUMPTION: introduced for subsequent computational modeling and not patient-derived.
PENDING/BLOCKED: cannot currently be verified or executed because the physical source imaging files are unavailable in the active workspace.

Why this change matters

At 0.38 g, for example, you cannot assume stress = 38% of the 1 g stress. The nonlinear constitutive model, constraints, pressure loading, geometry and deformation can make the relationship non-linear.

So:

Gravity level is prescribed. Stress is an outcome.

This is an important methodological correction.
